Minkyu Jeon

Ph.D. Candidate in Computer Science, Princeton University

I develop generative and representation-learning methods for scientific inverse problems, advised by Prof. Ellen D. Zhong. My current work focuses on agentic inference-time scaling for de novo protein design, where LLM agents run binder-design campaigns under a fixed GPU budget, and on heterogeneous cryo-EM reconstruction of conformational and compositional variability.

Previously, I was a research intern and contractor at Prescient Design (Genentech) and an Associate Computational Biologist at the Broad Institute of MIT and Harvard.

Research

Agentic AI × protein design

T-REX: agentic campaign control for de novo binder design

LLM agents decide when to rescue, explore, or exploit across six generation methods (four Protéina-Complexa search variants, BindCraft, BoltzGen), ProteinMPNN, and AlphaFold2; a deterministic controller validates and schedules every job.
